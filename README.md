# Wind-Turbine Blade — Material Selection & Eco-Audit (MSE 220)

Material selection study for a wind-turbine blade: screen candidate alloys against
mechanical and lifecycle criteria, then rank them to a defensible recommendation.

**Course:** MSE 220, Simon Fraser University · **Term:** Nov–Dec 2024 · **Team project**

## What I did

Mechanical and eco-audit analysis of 100+ materials in Granta EduPack — fracture
toughness, fatigue, cost and lifecycle impact — and implemented a penalty-function
method to auto-rank and filter candidates rather than eyeballing the selection charts.
Delivered data-backed recommendations from that ranking.

This was a team project; the material-selection and eco-audit analysis above is my
contribution.

## Opening these

The `.pdf` files are the viewable evidence and need nothing special — they are what this
repository publishes. The `.ces` (Granta EduPack project) and `.prd` (product record) files
require **Ansys Granta EduPack** and can embed personal metadata (a group member's SFU
student number is stored inside `FinalProjectMSE220.ces`), so they are **gitignored and kept
local** as source — not published. The per-material PDF exports above already carry their content.
